1. The customer/student attends one **free demo/trial session**.
2. If interested in joining after the demo, they proceed with admission.
3. Applicable registration/ID, uniform, and monthly fees are paid,
   according to the relevant branch and student category (Junior/Senior).

documents, medical certificates, registration forms, payment methods,
refund policies, cancellation policies, or trial restrictions.
