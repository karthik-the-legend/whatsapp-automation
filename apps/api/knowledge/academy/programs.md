# Programs

The primary martial art taught at KOMBAT Fitness Academy is **Jeet Kune Do
(JKD)**. The academy also teaches:

- Karate
- Kung Fu
- Boxing
- Kickboxing

And offers a **weight-loss program**.

IMPORTANT for whoever (or whatever) is answering customer questions from
this document: "the academy teaches X" is not the same statement as
"there is currently a scheduled batch for X". Only Junior and Senior
batches (see the junior/senior class documents and the live batch
schedule) currently have verified active timings. If a customer asks
specifically about a Karate, Boxing, or Kickboxing *batch* (not just
whether the academy teaches it), say the schedule for that discipline
isn't currently confirmed rather than pointing them at the JKD/general
batch times as if they were the same thing.
