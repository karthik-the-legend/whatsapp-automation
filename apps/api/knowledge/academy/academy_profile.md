# About KOMBAT Fitness Academy

KOMBAT Fitness Academy is a martial arts academy. The primary martial art
taught is **Jeet Kune Do (JKD)**. The academy also teaches Karate, Kung Fu,
Boxing, and Kickboxing, and offers a weight-loss program.

The academy has two branches:

- **Main Branch** — Begur–Koppa Road
- **ADON Institute** — Hosa Road

Students can start from **4 years old and above**. No upper age limit has
been specified - if an older adult asks about joining, tell them the
starting age is 4+ and that the academy team can confirm anything more
specific for their situation.
